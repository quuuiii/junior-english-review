=SET 1|分冊複習・第1冊|祈使句、現在進行式、be 動詞問答句、時間／場所介系詞、形容詞、名詞單複數、可數與不可數名詞、人稱代名詞、指示代名詞、Wh- 疑問詞、There is/are、連接詞 and/but
## SINGLE
1. Amy is in the kitchen. She is _____ a cake for her mother.
A. makes
B. make
C. making
D. to make
=C is 後面接現在分詞 V-ing，表示正在進行。
2. _____ the window, please. It's very hot in here.
A. Opening
B. Opens
C. Opened
D. Open
=D 祈使句以原形動詞開頭。
3. A: Is your brother at home? B: No, he _____. He is at school.
A. aren't
B. isn't
C. doesn't
D. not
=B 主詞 he 搭配 be 動詞 is，否定為 isn't。
4. The movie starts _____ seven o'clock.
A. at
B. on
C. in
D. of
=A 幾點鐘前面用 at。
5. Our English class is _____ Monday morning.
A. in
B. at
C. for
D. on
=D 星期幾（含星期幾的早上）前面用 on。
6. There are two _____ on the table. They are for Dad's birthday.
A. box
B. boxes
C. boxs
D. boxies
=B 名詞字尾為 x 時，複數加 es。
7. I want to eat some _____ for breakfast.
A. an egg
B. a bread
C. breads
D. bread
=D bread 是不可數名詞，前面可用 some，不加 a 也不加 s。
8. This is my sister. _____ name is Lily.
A. She
B. Her
C. Hers
D. He
=B 名詞 name 前面用所有格形容詞 her。
9. Look at those boys. _____ are my classmates.
A. Those
B. This
C. That
D. It
=A 指遠處的複數人物用 those，動詞 are 也呼應複數。
10. A: _____ is your English teacher? B: Mr. Brown.
A. What
B. Where
C. Who
D. When
=C 回答是人名，所以問「誰」用 Who。
11. A: _____ is the library? B: It's next to the post office.
A. Who
B. Where
C. When
D. What
=B 回答是位置，所以用 Where 問地點。
12. I have a pen _____ two books in my bag.
A. but
B. or
C. at
D. and
=D 兩個都是「我有」的東西，用 and 連接。
13. There _____ a cat under the chair.
A. are
B. am
C. be
D. is
=D a cat 是單數，用 There is。
14. What time _____ it? It's half past nine.
A. is
B. be
C. are
D. am
=A 問時間的固定句型是 What time is it?
15. A: Is that your dog? B: Yes, _____.
A. it isn't
B. they are
C. it is
D. I am
=C 問句主詞是 that，回答時用 it is。
16. Tina has two _____. They are very cute.
A. child
B. children
C. childs
D. childrens
=B child 的複數是不規則變化 children。
17. Please _____ late for school again, Ken.
A. don't be
B. not be
C. no
D. don't
=A 否定祈使句：Don't + 原形動詞；be 動詞原形為 be。
18. A: How are you today? B: _____
A. I'm Tom.
B. It's Monday.
C. Yes, I am.
D. I'm fine, thanks.
=D How are you? 是問候語，要回答近況。
19. Dan sees this sign in the park: "Don't feed the ducks." What does it mean?
A. The ducks are hungry.
B. People can't give food to the ducks.
C. People should look at the ducks.
D. The ducks can't swim here.
=B feed 是「餵食」，Don't feed 表示不可以餵。

## GROUP 25-27|對話|After School
Ben: Mom, where is my schoolbag?
Mom: It's on the sofa, Ben. Are your books in it?
Ben: Yes, they are. Oh, my pencil case is not here.
Mom: Look! There is a pencil case under the table. Is it yours?
Ben: No, it isn't. Mine is blue. That one is red.
Mom: Maybe it's your sister's. Ask her.
Ben: OK. Lisa, is this pencil case yours?
Lisa: Yes, it is! Thank you, Ben. Where is your pencil case?
Ben: Oh, it's in my pocket. I'm so silly!
25. Where is Ben's schoolbag?
A. On the sofa.
B. Under the table.
C. In his pocket.
D. In Lisa's room.
=A Mom 說 It's on the sofa。
26. Whose pencil case is red?
A. Ben's.
B. Mom's.
C. Lisa's.
D. The teacher's.
=C 紅色的鉛筆盒在 Lisa 回答 Yes, it is 之後確認是她的。
27. Why does Ben say, "I'm so silly!"?
A. He can't find his sister.
B. His books are not in his schoolbag.
C. He doesn't like the red pencil case.
D. His pencil case is in his pocket.
=D 他到處找鉛筆盒，卻發現在自己口袋裡，所以說自己很傻。
