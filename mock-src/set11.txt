## SINGLE
1. My brother is a(n) _____ person. He always makes everyone laugh at parties.
A. shy
B. funny
C. lazy
D. serious
=B 他總是讓大家哈哈大笑，表示他是風趣的人。
2. The test was so _____ that nobody could finish it on time.
A. easy
B. long
C. free
D. close
=B 考試太長，所以沒人能準時寫完。
3. We should save water. Every _____ counts.
A. drop
B. ground
C. mountain
D. step
=A 每一滴水都很重要，drop 是「一滴」。
4. A: Could you _____ me a favor? B: Sure. What is it?
A. do
B. make
C. take
D. get
=A do someone a favor 表示「幫某人一個忙」。
5. The weather forecast says it will be cloudy tomorrow, but the sun may _____ in the afternoon.
A. come out
B. go out
C. give out
D. turn out
=A 太陽出來用 come out。
6. Sara _____ on the sofa when the doorbell rang.
A. slept
B. is sleeping
C. was sleeping
D. has slept
=C 門鈴響的時候她正在睡覺，用過去進行式。
7. Jack is not only a good singer _____ a great dancer.
A. and
B. but also
C. so
D. or
=B not only A but also B 表示「不但……而且……」。
8. You'd better _____ more vegetables. They are good for your health.
A. eat
B. to eat
C. eating
D. ate
=A had better 後面接原形動詞。
9. This is the first time that I _____ a horse.
A. ride
B. rode
C. have ridden
D. am riding
=C the first time that 子句要用現在完成式。
10. A: Why don't we go hiking this Sunday? B: _____ I have to take care of my little brother.
A. That's a good idea.
B. I'd love to.
C. Sorry, I can't.
D. No problem.
=C 後面說明要照顧弟弟，表示婉拒。
11. The boy _____ is playing the guitar is my classmate.
A. which
B. whose
C. who
D. whom
=C 先行詞是人，作主詞，關係代名詞用 who。
12. Mom wants me to stay at home _____ it is too dangerous to go out.
A. although
B. because
C. unless
D. but
=B 因為出門太危險，所以媽媽要我留在家，表示原因。
13. The restaurant is famous _____ its delicious seafood.
A. for
B. of
C. with
D. at
=A be famous for 表示「以……聞名」。
14. Tom's room is _____ than mine, so he has more space.
A. large
B. larger
C. largest
D. more largely
=B 後面有 than，用比較級 larger。
15. After the earthquake, many people were _____ and needed help.
A. hurt
B. proud
C. fair
D. glad
=A 地震後許多人受傷需要幫助。
16. The singer was _____ by thousands of fans when she arrived at the airport.
A. welcome
B. welcomed
C. welcoming
D. welcomes
=B 歌手被歡迎，用被動語態 was welcomed。
17. Don't be afraid of making mistakes. We _____ from them.
A. learn
B. spend
C. borrow
D. wait
=A 我們從錯誤中學習，learn from 表示「從……學到」。
18. It's _____ of you to carry my heavy bag. Thank you very much.
A. kind
B. kindly
C. kindness
D. kinder
=A It is kind of you to V 表示「你真好心」。
19. A note on a refrigerator says: "Please do not open the door too long." What does the note ask people to do?
A. Open the door quickly.
B. Keep the door open.
C. Open the door as often as possible.
D. Close the door slowly.
=A 不要開門太久，意思是要快點開快點關。

## GROUP 23-24|短文|The Last Sandwich
Joe was walking home from school when he saw a thin dog near the market. The dog looked hungry and cold. Joe had a sandwich in his bag. It was the last one his mother had made for him, and he was hungry too. But he looked at the dog's eyes and knelt down. He broke the sandwich in half and gave one half to the dog. The dog ate it quickly and wagged its tail. Joe felt warm inside. The next day, he brought a bowl of food to the same place, but the dog was gone. He hoped that it had found a home.
23. Why did Joe give half of his sandwich to the dog?
A. His mother told him to.
B. He was not hungry.
C. He felt sorry for the dog.
D. He wanted to keep the dog.
=C 看到狗又餓又冷，他心生同情，分一半給牠。
24. What did Joe do the next day?
A. He found the dog a home.
B. He took the dog to the market.
C. He brought food for the dog.
D. He made another sandwich.
=C 隔天他帶了一碗食物到同一個地方。
## GROUP 25-27|對話|Choosing a Club
Rita: Have you decided which club to join, Paul?
Paul: Not yet. I like drawing, but my parents want me to join the science club.
Rita: Why do they want you to do that?
Paul: They think science will be more useful for my future.
Rita: I see. But shouldn't you choose what you love?
Paul: I think so, too. Still, I don't want to make them upset.
Rita: How about talking to them? Maybe they will understand if you show them your drawings.
Paul: That's a good idea. I'll try it tonight.
25. What does Paul like?
A. Science.
B. Music.
C. Drawing.
D. Sports.
=C Paul 說他喜歡畫畫。
26. Why do Paul's parents want him to join the science club?
A. They think it is useful for his future.
B. They are scientists.
C. They don't like drawing.
D. The club is free.
=A 父母認為科學對他的未來比較有用。
27. What will Paul probably do tonight?
A. Join the science club.
B. Talk to his parents.
C. Quit drawing.
D. Call Rita.
=B Paul 說今晚會試著跟父母談談，並讓他們看他的畫。

## GROUP 35-37|短文|Sleep and Learning
Many students stay up late to study before an exam. They think that more study time means better grades. However, scientists have found that this may not be true. Sleep plays an important role in how our brain stores information. When we sleep, the brain organizes what we learned during the day. Students who sleep well after studying can remember about 20% more than those who do not. In another study, students who slept only four hours a night had more trouble paying attention in class. Therefore, experts suggest that teenagers get eight to ten hours of sleep each night.
35. What is the main idea of the passage?
A. Students should study more at night.
B. Sleep helps students learn better.
C. Teenagers like staying up late.
D. Exams are harder than before.
=B 全文說明睡眠有助於大腦儲存資訊，幫助學習。
36. What happens when we sleep?
A. We forget what we learned.
B. We eat less.
C. Our brain organizes what we learned.
D. We feel hungry.
=C 睡覺時，大腦整理白天學到的東西。
37. How many hours of sleep do experts suggest for teenagers?
A. Four to six.
B. Six to eight.
C. Eight to ten.
D. More than twelve.
=C 專家建議青少年每晚睡八到十小時。
## GROUP 38-43|填空|The Sea Turtle
Last summer, my family visited a beach in Pingtung. We went there to see sea turtles __38__ eggs on the sand. Sea turtles are in danger because people __39__ their eggs and dirty the ocean. Many of them die by eating plastic bags, which look like jellyfish. The guide told us, "If you see a sea turtle, please keep a distance and __40__ it alone." At night, we saw a mother turtle come out of the water. She dug a hole and laid about one hundred eggs. It was a __41__ moment that I will never forget. __42__ the turtles need our help, we should do our best to protect them. After this trip, I stopped __43__ plastic bags.
38.
A. lay
B. laying
C. laid
D. lays
=B see + 受詞 + V-ing，表示看到海龜正在產卵。
39.
A. steal
B. steals
C. stole
D. stealing
=A people 是複數，現在式動詞用 steal。
40.
A. leave
B. leaves
C. leaving
D. to leave

41.
A. touch
B. touched
C. touching
D. touches

42.
A. Because
B. Although
C. Unless
D. So
=A 因為海龜需要我們的幫助，所以我們應該盡力保護牠們，用 Because 引導原因。
43.
A. use
B. to use
C. using
D. used
=C stop + V-ing 表示停止做某事。
