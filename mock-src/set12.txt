## SINGLE
1. Please speak _____. The baby is sleeping.
A. quietly
B. quiet
C. quieter
D. quietness
=A 修飾動詞 speak 要用副詞 quietly。
2. The old man lives _____, so his children visit him every weekend.
A. alone
B. lonely
C. among
D. against
=A live alone 表示獨自生活。
3. I'm sorry. I didn't _____ to hurt your feelings.
A. wish
B. mean
C. hope
D. guess
=B didn't mean to V 表示「不是故意要……」。
4. Our teacher gave us a lot of homework, _____ we couldn't go to the party.
A. because
B. so
C. but
D. if
=B 功課很多是原因，不能去派對是結果，用 so。
5. A: Do you mind if I open the window? B: _____ It's too hot in here.
A. Yes, I do.
B. No, go ahead.
C. I'd rather not.
D. That's too bad.
=B Do you mind 的回答，No 表示不介意，可以開窗。
6. The students were asked to _____ the classroom before they left.
A. clean up
B. look up
C. give up
D. pick up
=A clean up 表示打掃乾淨。
7. By the end of this year, my father _____ in this company for twenty years.
A. works
B. will work
C. has worked
D. will have worked
=D By the end of this year 搭配未來完成式 will have worked。
8. It is _____ to eat too much junk food.
A. health
B. healthy
C. unhealthy
D. healthily
=C 吃太多垃圾食物是不健康的，用 unhealthy。
9. I don't know _____ to say. I'm so surprised.
A. what
B. which
C. who
D. where
=A 驚訝得不知道要說什麼，用 what to say。
10. The movie was so touching _____ many people cried.
A. as
B. that
C. but
D. which
=B so + 形容詞 + that 子句。
11. Mary is the girl _____ father is a famous doctor.
A. who
B. which
C. whose
D. whom
=C Mary 的爸爸，表示所有關係，用 whose。
12. I'd like to buy a _____ for my little sister. She loves playing with it.
A. toy
B. sofa
C. coin
D. basket
=A 小妹喜歡玩，所以想買玩具。
13. If you _____ harder, you would pass the exam.
A. study
B. studied
C. will study
D. had studied
=B 與現在事實相反的假設，if 子句用過去式 studied。
14. The bridge, _____ was built in 1990, is now the symbol of our city.
A. who
B. what
C. which
D. where
=C 先行詞是物 The bridge，非限定用法，用 which。
15. She has _____ finished her work, so she can go home now.
A. yet
B. just
C. ever
D. never
=B has just finished 表示剛剛完成。
16. The little girl is afraid _____ dogs.
A. at
B. in
C. of
D. to
=C be afraid of 表示「害怕……」。
17. Tom ran so fast that he _____ the bus.
A. missed
B. caught
C. lost
D. left
=B 他跑得很快，所以趕上了公車。
18. Neither my sister nor I _____ interested in sports.
A. am
B. is
C. are
D. be
=A Neither...nor 的動詞與靠近它的主詞 I 一致，用 am。
19. A sign on a lake says: "Danger! Thin ice." What does the sign mean?
A. People can skate here.
B. People should not walk on the ice.
C. The lake is closed in summer.
D. The ice is very thick.
=B Thin ice 表示冰很薄，很危險，不應在上面行走。

## GROUP 23-24|短文|The Magic Pencil Case
Amy had an old pencil case. It was torn and had a big ink stain. Her classmates often laughed at it, and she wanted a new one. One day, her grandmother told her, "That pencil case was mine when I was your age. I used it for six years, and I learned everything I know with it." Amy was surprised. She looked at the pencil case again. It no longer looked old to her; it looked special. From then on, she took good care of it. She even sewed up the hole by herself. She learned that the value of something is not in how new it looks but in the memories it holds.
23. Why did Amy want a new pencil case at first?
A. Her grandmother gave her money.
B. Her friends laughed at her old one.
C. She lost her old one.
D. Her old one was too small.
=B 同學常嘲笑她那個破舊的鉛筆盒，所以她想換新的。
24. What did Amy learn?
A. Old things are always better.
B. New things are not important.
C. Memories give things value.
D. She should sew every day.
=C 事物的價值在於它所承載的回憶，而非外表是否嶄新。
## GROUP 25-27|對話|At the Doctor's
Doctor: What seems to be the problem?
Kim: I have a terrible headache, and I've been coughing since yesterday.
Doctor: Do you have a fever?
Kim: I think so. I feel hot, but I haven't taken my temperature.
Doctor: Let me check. Yes, it's 38.5°C. How long have you been sleeping at night?
Kim: Only about four hours. I've been preparing for an exam.
Doctor: That's not enough. You should rest more. I'll give you some medicine. Take it three times a day after meals.
Kim: Thank you, doctor. Should I go to school tomorrow?
Doctor: No. Stay at home for two days and drink plenty of water.
25. What is wrong with Kim?
A. She has a stomachache.
B. She hurt her leg.
C. She has a headache, a cough, and a fever.
D. She can't sleep because of noise.
=C Kim 頭痛、咳嗽，醫生量到體溫 38.5°C，表示發燒。
26. Why does Kim sleep so little?
A. She works at night.
B. She is preparing for an exam.
C. She watches TV.
D. She feels too hot.
=B 她一直在準備考試，每晚只睡四小時。
27. What does the doctor tell Kim to do?
A. Go to school tomorrow.
B. Take medicine before meals.
C. Stay at home and drink water.
D. Do more exercise.
=C 醫生要她在家休息兩天並多喝水。

## GROUP 35-37|短文|Street Musicians
Every weekend, a group of young people play music on a busy street in the city center. They don't have a stage, and their instruments are simple: a guitar, a drum, and a small keyboard. At first, few people stopped to listen, but they kept playing. Slowly, a crowd began to form. Now, people come from all over the city to hear them. One of the players said, "We don't do this for money. We want to bring happiness to people who are in a hurry." Some people put coins in their hat, while others just smile and say thank you. The city government has even asked them to play at the New Year concert.
35. What is the main purpose of the young people?
A. To earn a lot of money.
B. To become famous singers.
C. To bring happiness to busy people.
D. To teach music to children.
=C 他們說不是為了錢，而是想帶給匆忙的人快樂。
36. What happened after the group kept playing?
A. The street was closed.
B. A crowd began to form.
C. They lost their instruments.
D. They moved to another city.
=B 一開始很少人停下來，但慢慢地聚集了群眾。
37. What can we infer from the last sentence?
A. The band is becoming well known.
B. The band is too noisy.
C. The government doesn't like the band.
D. The band plays only at New Year.
=A 市政府邀請他們在跨年演唱會表演，可見他們已有名氣。
## GROUP 38-43|填空|A Good Habit
My uncle is a person who gets up very early every morning. He says that this is a good habit that has __38__ him a lot. He goes jogging for an hour and then __39__ breakfast for the whole family. When I was little, I often wondered __40__ he didn't feel sleepy. He said, "In the morning, the air is fresh, and my mind is clear." Last year, I decided to follow him. At first, it was __41__ for me to get up at five. But after a few weeks, I got used to it. Now I feel __42__ and have more energy during the day. I think developing good habits can make our lives much __43__.
38.
A. help
B. helped
C. helping
D. helps
=B has 後面接過去分詞 helped，構成現在完成式。
39.
A. make
B. makes
C. making
D. made
=B 主詞是 he，現在簡單式動詞加 s。
40.
A. why
B. what
C. where
D. whose
=A wonder why he didn't feel sleepy，表示納悶為什麼不覺得睏。
41.
A. hard
B. hardly
C. harder
D. hardest
=A it is hard for me to V，hard 是形容詞，表示「困難的」。
42.
A. refresh
B. refreshed
C. refreshing
D. refreshes
=B feel + 形容詞，主詞是人，表示「感到精神煥發」，用 refreshed。
43.
A. good
B. better
C. best
D. well
=B make + 受詞 + 形容詞，表示「使生活變得更好」，用比較級 better。
