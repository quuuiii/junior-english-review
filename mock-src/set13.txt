=SET 13|會考模擬（三）|第1～6冊全範圍：詞彙、文法、對話、圖表與短文閱讀、綜合測驗
## SINGLE
1. The tour guide asked us to _____ together so that no one would get lost.
A. stay
B. leave
C. spend
D. wonder
=A 為了不走失，導遊要大家待在一起。
2. My mother is very _____ about my health. She always makes me eat breakfast.
A. serious
B. worried
C. surprised
D. excited
=B 她擔心我的健康，所以總是要我吃早餐。
3. The price of vegetables _____ a lot after the typhoon last week.
A. went up
B. went out
C. went on
D. went by
=A 颱風後菜價上漲，go up 表示上升。
4. Neither of the two shirts _____ me. They are both too small.
A. fit
B. fits
C. fitting
D. to fit
=B Neither of the two 當單數處理，現在式用 fits。
5. A: How about going to the night market tonight? B: _____. I have a lot of homework to do.
A. Why not
B. Sounds great
C. I'd rather not
D. Of course
=C 後面說有很多功課，表示婉拒，用 I'd rather not。
6. It took Tom two years _____ the book.
A. write
B. to write
C. writing
D. wrote
=B It takes + 時間 + to V。
7. I enjoy _____ to music while I am doing my homework.
A. listen
B. to listen
C. listening
D. listened
=C enjoy 後面接動名詞 listening。
8. The cookies _____ by my grandmother taste wonderful.
A. make
B. made
C. making
D. are made
=B 過去分詞後置修飾 cookies，表示「奶奶做的餅乾」。
9. He is the only person _____ I can talk to about this problem.
A. who
B. what
C. whose
D. which
=A 先行詞是人，用 who；what 不接先行詞。
10. We must hurry. The movie _____ start in ten minutes.
A. is going to
B. has to
C. used to
D. would
=A 電影十分鐘後就要開始，表示即將發生的事，用 be going to。
11. Mr. Chang looks angry. I wonder _____ he is upset.
A. why
B. what
C. which
D. whether
=A wonder why 表示納悶為什麼他生氣。
12. Sam is _____ than any other student in his class.
A. tall
B. taller
C. the tallest
D. more tall
=B 與班上任何其他學生比較，用比較級 taller。
13. Ann asked me _____ I could lend her my notes.
A. that
B. if
C. what
D. how
=B 間接問句表示「能不能」，用 if。
14. I can't find my keys. I think I _____ them at the restaurant.
A. leave
B. left
C. have leave
D. am leaving
=B 鑰匙是之前遺留的，用過去式 left。
15. The more careful you are, the _____ mistakes you will make.
A. few
B. fewer
C. less
D. fewest
=B mistakes 是可數名詞，用 fewer；the + 比較級 ..., the + 比較級 ...。
16. Please give me some advice. I don't know _____ to do.
A. what
B. who
C. how
D. which
=A 不知道要做什麼，用 what to do。
17. Mr. Lin was so tired that he fell asleep _____ he got on the bus.
A. as soon as
B. even if
C. unless
D. as long as
=A as soon as 表示「一……就……」。
18. This is a story _____ happened in a small village many years ago.
A. who
B. whom
C. which
D. where
=C 先行詞是物 a story，作主詞，用 which。
19. A label on a medicine bottle says: "Keep out of reach of children." What does it mean?
A. Children should take the medicine.
B. Children should not touch the medicine.
C. The medicine is only for children.
D. The medicine tastes sweet.
=B 要放在小孩拿不到的地方，所以小孩不該碰。

## GROUP 23-24|短文|Grandpa's Watch
Grandpa had an old watch that he wore every day. It did not tell time correctly anymore, and it was scratched. My father asked, "Why don't you buy a new one?" Grandpa smiled and said, "This watch was a gift from your mother. She gave it to me when we got married, forty years ago." My father fell silent. A week later, he took the watch to a shop and had it repaired. When he gave it back to Grandpa, Grandpa's eyes were full of tears. "Thank you, son," he said. "Now it can keep going for another forty years."
23. Why didn't Grandpa buy a new watch?
A. He couldn't afford one.
B. The watch was a special gift.
C. He didn't need a watch.
D. His son told him not to.
=B 手錶是太太送的結婚禮物，對他有特別意義。
24. How did Grandpa feel when he got the watch back?
A. Moved.
B. Angry.
C. Bored.
D. Worried.
=A 他眼中泛淚並道謝，表示很感動。
## GROUP 25-27|對話|Joining the Team
Coach: Welcome to the basketball team, Ryan. Have you played before?
Ryan: Yes. I played for my elementary school team for three years.
Coach: Great. We have practice every Tuesday and Thursday after school, from 4:30 to 6:00.
Ryan: Can I miss a practice if I have a test?
Coach: Of course. Just let me know in advance. By the way, do you have a pair of basketball shoes?
Ryan: Not yet. I plan to buy a pair this weekend.
Coach: Good. Remember to also bring a water bottle and a towel to every practice.
Ryan: Got it. Thank you, Coach.
25. How long is each practice?
A. Thirty minutes.
B. One hour.
C. One and a half hours.
D. Two hours.
=C 從 4:30 到 6:00，共一個半小時。
26. What should Ryan do if he has a test on a practice day?
A. Come anyway.
B. Quit the team.
C. Tell the coach in advance.
D. Ask his friends to play for him.
=C 教練說有考試可以缺席，但要事先告知。
27. What does Ryan plan to do this weekend?
A. Play in a game.
B. Buy basketball shoes.
C. Take a test.
D. Visit his elementary school.
=B 他打算週末買一雙籃球鞋。

## GROUP 35-37|短文|Honey Bees
Honey bees are small, but they play a big role in our lives. About one third of the food we eat depends on bees, because they carry pollen from flower to flower. This helps plants make fruits and seeds. However, the number of bees around the world has been dropping. Scientists say that pesticides and the loss of flowers are two main reasons. Some cities have started to help. They plant more flowers in parks and along roads, and they limit the use of pesticides. Ordinary people can also help. For example, you can grow flowers on your balcony or avoid using chemicals in your garden.
35. Why are bees important?
A. They make people healthy.
B. They help plants make fruits and seeds.
C. They kill other insects.
D. They clean flowers.
=B 蜜蜂傳花粉，幫助植物結果和結種子。
36. What is one reason bees are disappearing?
A. Too many flowers.
B. Pesticides.
C. Cold weather.
D. Too many people.
=B 科學家指出殺蟲劑是蜜蜂減少的主因之一。
37. What can ordinary people do to help bees?
A. Collect honey.
B. Grow flowers.
C. Move to the city.
D. Use more chemicals.
=B 民眾可以在陽台種花來幫助蜜蜂。
