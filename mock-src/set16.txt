=SET 16|會考模擬（六）|第1～6冊全範圍：詞彙、文法、對話、圖表與短文閱讀、綜合測驗（含素養題材）
## SINGLE
1. Please _____ your seat belt. The plane is going to take off soon.
A. fasten
B. fix
C. cover
D. hang
=A fasten a seat belt 表示繫好安全帶。
2. The little boy was _____ when he heard a strange noise in the dark.
A. frightened
B. frightening
C. frighten
D. frightens
=A 主詞是人，表示「感到害怕的」，用過去分詞 frightened。
3. Sandy has a strong _____ for music. She started playing the violin at the age of four.
A. passion
B. pressure
C. promise
D. power
=A have a passion for 表示對……充滿熱情。
4. The news that our school team won the game _____ everyone happy.
A. made
B. let
C. got
D. found
=A make + 受詞 + 形容詞，表示「使……」。
5. You should _____ your teeth after every meal to keep them healthy.
A. brush
B. paint
C. wash
D. clean up
=A 刷牙用 brush。
6. The new shopping mall, _____ opened last month, is always crowded.
A. that
B. who
C. which
D. where
=C 非限定子句，逗號後面不能用 that，先行詞是物，用 which。
7. I have no idea _____ I should do to improve my English.
A. what
B. that
C. who
D. whose
=A what I should do 作 idea 的內容，表示「我應該做什麼」。
8. Jim spent two hours _____ the report last night.
A. write
B. to write
C. writing
D. wrote
=C spend + 時間 + V-ing。
9. The movie is worth _____ again.
A. to see
B. seeing
C. see
D. seen
=B be worth + V-ing。
10. A: I got an A on my science test! B: _____! You studied so hard.
A. That's too bad
B. Congratulations
C. Take it easy
D. Never mind
=B 對方考了 A，恭喜用 Congratulations。
11. By the time I got to the party, all the cake _____.
A. has eaten
B. had been eaten
C. was eating
D. is eaten
=B 到達之前蛋糕就已被吃完，用過去完成式被動 had been eaten。
12. Tom, _____ the bus, was late for school.
A. missing
B. missed
C. misses
D. to miss
=A 分詞構句，Tom 主動錯過公車，用現在分詞 missing（= Because he missed the bus）。
13. Not only Mary but also her brothers _____ going to the party.
A. is
B. are
C. am
D. be
=B not only...but also 的動詞與靠近的 her brothers 一致，用 are。
14. The girl was _____ shy that she couldn't say a word in front of the class.
A. too
B. such
C. very
D. so
=D so + 形容詞 + that 子句。
15. If I _____ a million dollars, I would travel around the world.
A. have
B. had
C. will have
D. would have
=B 與現在事實相反的假設，if 子句用過去式 had。
16. We can see that the earth _____ warmer and warmer these days.
A. gets
B. got
C. is getting
D. has got
=C 近來地球越來越暖，用現在進行式表示正在持續的變化。
17. It's difficult for me _____ the meaning of this sentence.
A. understand
B. understood
C. understanding
D. to understand
=D It is + 形容詞 + for + 人 + to V。
18. My cousin asked me whether I _____ him with his homework.
A. help
B. helped
C. could help
D. am helping
=C 間接問句詢問「能不能幫他」，用 could help 表示可能性。
19. A poster in the school says: "Think before you share online." What does it want students to do?
A. Share everything quickly.
B. Check information before posting.
C. Stop using the Internet.
D. Share only with teachers.
=B 先思考再分享，要確認資訊後再發佈。

## GROUP 23-24|短文|The Wrong Direction
Ben was driving to his friend's wedding when his GPS told him to turn left. But Ben knew a shorter way, so he ignored it and turned right. Ten minutes later, he found that the road was closed. He had to go back and take the way the GPS had shown. By the time he arrived, the wedding had already started. Everyone turned and looked at him. Ben felt embarrassed. Afterwards, his friend laughed and said, "It's okay. Even smart people sometimes need help from a machine." Ben learned that being too sure of himself is not always a good thing.
23. Why was Ben late for the wedding?
A. His car broke down.
B. He ignored the GPS and took a closed road.
C. He forgot the address.
D. He stopped to buy a gift.
=B 他不聽導航，選了一條封閉的路，只好繞回來。
24. What did Ben learn?
A. GPS is always wrong.
B. Weddings start late.
C. He shouldn't be too sure of himself.
D. He should not drive alone.
=C 他學到太過自信不一定是好事。
## GROUP 25-27|對話|Online Shopping
Eric: Did you see the sale on the website? Everything is 50% off today!
Fiona: Really? That sounds too good to be true.
Eric: It's a well-known brand, so it should be real. I'm going to buy a pair of shoes.
Fiona: Be careful. Last week, my friend bought a bag from a similar website. It never arrived.
Eric: Oh no. How can I know if the website is safe?
Fiona: First, check whether the web address begins with "https." Second, read other customers' reviews. If many people complain, don't buy.
Eric: Good advice. I'll check before I pay.
25. What did Fiona's friend experience?
A. She bought shoes at half price.
B. The bag she ordered never arrived.
C. She was given a free bag.
D. The website closed.
=B 她朋友買的包包從未送達。
26. What does Fiona mean by "too good to be true"?
A. She is sure the sale is real.
B. She doubts the sale is real.
C. She will buy something.
D. She wants to share the news.
=B 太好了反而不像真的，表示她對促銷有所懷疑。
27. What should Eric do before paying?
A. Read reviews and check the web address.
B. Ask Fiona to buy for him.
C. Call the brand.
D. Wait for a week.
=A Fiona 建議他先檢查網址，並閱讀其他顧客的評價。

## GROUP 35-37|短文|Fake News
Every day we see news on social media, but not all of it is true. Fake news can spread quickly because it is often shocking or exciting, and people share it before thinking. In 2013, a false report said that a famous company was going to close, and its stock price fell sharply within an hour. Later, people found that the report was made by someone who wanted to make money. To avoid being fooled, experts suggest we ask three questions: Who wrote it? Where did the information come from? Do other reliable news sources report the same thing? If we cannot answer these questions, we should not share it.
35. Why does fake news spread quickly?
A. It is always long.
B. It is shocking or exciting, so people share it quickly.
C. It is written by experts.
D. It is free.
=B 假新聞常令人震驚或興奮，大家沒細想就分享。
36. What happened after the false report about the company?
A. The company opened a new shop.
B. The stock price went up.
C. The company's stock price fell sharply.
D. The company was sold.
=C 假報導出現後，該公司股價在一小時內暴跌。
37. According to the passage, what should we do if we can't check the source?
A. Share it with friends.
B. Not share it.
C. Write a comment.
D. Believe it.
=B 如果無法回答這些問題，就不應該分享。

I told him that he __41__ have those two hours if he cut down on game time. A few weeks later, he told me he had started to learn the guitar, and he felt __42__ than before. The lesson is clear: if we use our time wisely, we can do things __43__ really matter to us.
