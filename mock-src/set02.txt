=SET 2|分冊複習・第2冊|現在簡單式、過去簡單式（be 動詞）、助動詞 can 問答句、數量形容詞、When／Which 問答句、How many／How much、連接詞 or、詢問時間和星期、序數、頻率副詞
## SINGLE
1. My father _____ up at six every morning and makes breakfast for us.
A. get
B. getting
C. gets
D. to get
=C 主詞 My father 是第三人稱單數，現在簡單式動詞加 s。
2. A: _____ you play the piano? B: Yes, I can. I play it every day.
A. Do
B. Are
C. Does
D. Can
=D 回答 Yes, I can，所以問句是 Can you...?
3. The weather _____ cold yesterday, so we stayed at home.
A. was
B. is
C. were
D. be
=A yesterday 表示過去，the weather 是單數，用 was。
4. There is _____ juice in the bottle. Let's buy some more.
A. many
B. any
C. a few
D. little
=D juice 是不可數名詞，且「所以要多買一些」表示幾乎沒有，用 little。
5. A: _____ do you have your piano lesson? B: On Wednesday afternoon.
A. Where
B. Which
C. Who
D. When
=D 回答是時間，用 When 詢問。
6. A: _____ books do you have in your bag? B: Five.
A. How many
B. How much
C. How long
D. How old
=A books 是可數名詞，問數量用 How many。
7. A: _____ do you like better, tea or coffee? B: Tea, please.
A. What
B. Which
C. When
D. Who
=B 在 tea 與 coffee 兩者之間選擇，用 Which。
8. You can take the bus _____ ride a bike to school. Both ways are fast.
A. and
B. but
C. so
D. or
=D 兩種方式選一種，用 or。
9. February is the _____ month of the year.
A. two
B. second
C. twice
D. twelve
=B 表示順序用序數 second。
10. Ann _____ goes to the library on Sundays. She goes there almost every week.
A. never
B. seldom
C. often
D. hardly
=C almost every week 表示頻率高，用 often。
11. A: What day is it today? B: It's _____. Tomorrow is Saturday.
A. Thursday
B. Sunday
C. Friday
D. Monday
=C 明天是星期六，所以今天是星期五。
12. Peter wasn't at school yesterday. He _____ sick.
A. is
B. weren't
C. be
D. was
=D yesterday 表示過去，he 搭配 was。
13. We _____ any money in our pockets now. Let's ask Dad for some.
A. don't have
B. doesn't have
C. have
D. has
=A 後面有 any 且語意為「沒有錢」，主詞 We 用 don't have。
14. Does Linda have _____ brothers?
A. some
B. a
C. any
D. much
=C 疑問句中表示「一些」用 any，brothers 是複數可數名詞。
15. A: What time does the movie start? B: At a _____ to eight.
A. half
B. quarter
C. past
D. clock
=B a quarter to eight 是七點四十五分。
16. I have _____ friends in this city, so I feel lonely.
A. a little
B. a few
C. little
D. few
=D 因為覺得孤單，表示朋友幾乎沒有，用 few（可數、否定意味）。
17. A: Which bag is yours, the red one or the blue one? B: _____
A. It's Mary's.
B. Yes, it is.
C. I like red.
D. The blue one.
=D Which 問句要從選項中選一個來回答。
18. They _____ in Japan last year. Now they are in Taiwan.
A. are
B. was
C. were
D. be
=C last year 表示過去，主詞 They 用 were。
19. The sign in front of the shop says: "Open 10 a.m. - 8 p.m. every day except Tuesday." When is the shop closed?
A. Every day.
B. On Tuesday.
C. On weekends.
D. At 9 a.m. only.
=B except Tuesday 表示星期二以外都開，所以星期二休息。

20. How long is the book sale?
A. One day.
B. Two days.
C. Three days.
D. Four days.

21. Ben buys two comic books and one dictionary. How much does he pay?
A. $6
B. $7
C. $8
D. $12
=B 兩本漫畫 $2 加一本字典 $5，共 $7。
22. What can people do with the money from the sale?
A. Buy more books for Green Library.
B. Help the children's hospital.
C. Give a free storybook to everyone.

## GROUP 23-24|短文|Kevin's Saturday
Kevin usually gets up at eight o'clock on Saturday. After breakfast, he often plays basketball with his friends in the park. They play for two hours. Then he goes home and takes a shower. In the afternoon, he sometimes does his homework or watches TV. But today is different. It is rainy, and Kevin is at home all day. He is bored, so he reads an English book. It is very interesting, and he can't stop reading.
23. What does Kevin usually do on Saturday morning?
A. He does his homework.
B. He watches TV.
C. He reads an English book.
D. He plays basketball.
=D 文中說 he often plays basketball with his friends。
24. Why does Kevin read an English book today?
A. It is rainy, and he is bored.
B. His teacher asks him to read it.
C. His friends are not at home.
D. He needs it for his homework.
=A 今天下雨，他在家很無聊，所以讀書。
## GROUP 25-27|對話|Planning a Party
Amy: Lucy's birthday is next Friday. Let's have a party for her.
Jack: Good idea! Which place is better, her house or the park?
Amy: The park is better. It's big, and the weather is nice in October.
Jack: OK. What time is the party?
Amy: At four o'clock. School ends at three, so we have enough time.
Jack: How many people are coming?
Amy: About ten. Can you bring some drinks?
Jack: Sure. How many bottles do we need?
Amy: Two or three bottles are enough. Oh, and I can make a cake.
Jack: Great! Lucy will love it.
25. Where will they have the party?
A. At Lucy's house.
B. At school.
C. In the park.
D. At Jack's house.
=C Amy 說公園比較好，兩人決定在公園。
26. What will Jack do for the party?
A. Make a cake.
B. Bring some drinks.
C. Buy a present.
D. Call ten people.
=B Amy 請 Jack 帶一些飲料。
27. Which is true about the party?
A. It starts at three.
B. It is on Saturday.
C. About ten people are coming.
D. They need ten bottles.
=C Jack 問人數，Amy 回答 About ten。
## GROUP 28-29|規則|Swimming Pool Rules
Swimming Pool Rules
1. Take a shower before you swim.
2. Do not run around the pool.
3. Children under ten must swim with an adult.
4. Do not eat or drink in the water.
5. The pool is open from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. It is closed on the first Monday of every month.
28. Who must swim with an adult?
A. Anyone who can't swim.
B. Teachers.
C. Children under ten.
D. People who are ten or older.
=C 規則 3 說十歲以下的小孩要有大人陪同。
29. Which is NOT allowed at the pool?
A. Taking a shower before swimming.
B. Swimming at five o'clock.
C. Going there on the second Monday of the month.
D. Running around the pool.
=D 規則 2 禁止在池邊奔跑；A、B、C 都是允許的。

Emma
33. Why does Emma write to Sally?
A. To ask her to bring a cake.
B. To invite her to a party.
C. To say sorry to her.

D. Seven.
=B 文中說 There are five of us。
## GROUP 35-37|短文|Grandpa's Garden
My grandpa has a small garden behind his house. He grows vegetables and fruit there. There are many tomatoes, some carrots, and a few apple trees. Grandpa works in the garden every morning. He often says, "A garden needs your time." This summer, I am at his house for two weeks. It is very hot, but I like the days here. I help him water the plants and pick the tomatoes. In the evening, we eat fresh vegetables for dinner. They taste so good! Next year, I want to come here again.
35. Where is Grandpa's garden?
A. In front of his house.
B. Behind his house.
C. Next to a school.
D. In the park.
=B 文中第一句 behind his house。
36. How often does Grandpa work in the garden?
A. Every evening.
B. Once a week.
C. Every morning.
D. On summer days only.
=C Grandpa works in the garden every morning。
37. What can we learn about the writer?
A. He grows apple trees.
B. He is staying with Grandpa for two weeks.
C. He doesn't like fresh vegetables.
D. He never helps Grandpa.
=B 文中說 I am at his house for two weeks。
## GROUP 38-43|填空|A Trip to Kenting
I am in Kenting with my family now. We are here __38__ three days. Today we are at the beach. The sea __39__ blue, and the sand is white. My little sister __40__ swim, but she likes to play in the sand. Tomorrow we visit a famous night market. There __41__ always a lot of people there, so we must wait a long time for food. I want ten pieces of fried chicken, __42__ my brother wants two bowls of noodles. __43__ is your favorite place in Taiwan? Please write to me.
38.
A. for
B. in
C. at
D. on
=A for three days 表示「為期三天」。
39.
A. am
B. are
C. be
D. is
=D the sea 是單數，現在式 be 動詞用 is。
40.
A. doesn't can
B. can not to
C. not can
D. can't
=D can 的否定是 can't，後面接原形動詞 swim。
41.
A. is
B. am
C. are
D. be
=C a lot of people 指許多人，動詞用複數 are。
42.
A. or
B. and
C. so
D. if
=B 我想吃雞排，弟弟想吃麵，是並列關係，用 and。
43.
A. What
B. Where
C. When
D. Who
=A What is your favorite place...? 是「你最喜歡的地方是什麼」。
