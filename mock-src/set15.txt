=SET 15|會考模擬（五）|第1～6冊全範圍：詞彙、文法、對話、圖表與短文閱讀、綜合測驗
## SINGLE
1. The coach told the players to _____ hard before the big game.
A. practice
B. practices
C. practicing
D. practiced
=A tell + 人 + to V，to 後面接原形動詞 practice。
2. A: Would you like to have some more soup? B: _____. I'm full.
A. Yes, please
B. No, thanks
C. Sure
D. Of course
=B 說「我飽了」，表示婉拒，用 No, thanks。
3. The singer became _____ after she won the contest.
A. fame
B. famous
C. famously
D. fames
=B become 是連綴動詞，後面接形容詞 famous。
4. I can't hear the teacher. Please turn the radio _____.
A. up
B. down
C. in
D. on
=B 聽不到老師說話，是因為收音機太吵，所以要把音量轉小，用 turn down。
5. My uncle _____ in New York for ten years before he moved back to Taiwan.
A. lives
B. lived
C. has lived
D. is living
=B before he moved back 是過去，主要子句也用過去式 lived。
6. Mom said, "Don't forget _____ the lights before you leave."
A. turn off
B. turning off
C. to turn off
D. turned off
=C forget to V 表示忘記去做某事，這裡是提醒別忘了關燈。
7. This is the museum _____ we visited last summer.
A. where
B. who
C. which
D. whose
=C 先行詞是物，作 visited 的受詞，用 which；若用 where，後面不應再有 visited 的受詞。
8. She talked _____ she knew everything about the topic.
A. as if
B. so that
C. even though
D. as long as
=A as if 表示「好像……」，她說得好像什麼都知道。
9. It is important for students _____ enough sleep.
A. get
B. to get
C. getting
D. got
=B It is + 形容詞 + for + 人 + to V。
10. The boy is _____ to reach the book on the top shelf.
A. enough tall
B. tall enough
C. too tall
D. so tall
=B 形容詞 + enough + to V，enough 要放在形容詞後面。
11. A: What did the teacher say? B: She asked us _____ our books.
A. open
B. opening
C. to open
D. opened
=C ask + 人 + to V。
12. When I _____ the gym, I saw some boys playing basketball.
A. walk
B. walked into
C. walking into
D. have walked into
=B 兩個過去動作依序發生，walked into 後面直接接地點。
13. No one knows _____ the thief ran away.
A. when
B. that
C. which
D. what
=A 間接問句缺少時間，用 when 表示「什麼時候」。
14. The shirt is _____ expensive for me to buy.
A. too
B. very
C. so
D. enough
=A too + 形容詞 + for + 人 + to V。
15. Jenny is proud _____ her son. He won the first prize.
A. in
B. of
C. at
D. with
=B be proud of 表示「以……為傲」。
16. If it hadn't been for your help, I _____ the project on time.
A. can't finish
B. couldn't have finished
C. won't finish
D. didn't finish
=B 與過去事實相反的假設，主要子句用 would/could have + 過去分詞。
17. The workers have been working here _____ eight o'clock this morning.
A. for
B. since
C. in
D. at
=B since 後面接過去的時間點 eight o'clock。
18. Lisa is really good _____ solving math problems.
A. in
B. at
C. for
D. of
=B be good at 表示「擅長……」。
19. A sign at a bus stop says: "Please let passengers get off first." What does it ask people to do?
A. Get on the bus quickly.
B. Wait until others leave the bus.
C. Stand on the bus.
D. Pay before getting on.
=B 請讓乘客先下車，所以要等別人下車後再上車。

## GROUP 23-24|短文|The Shy Cat
When Ms. Lee adopted a cat named Milo, he was very shy. He hid under the bed for days and only came out at night. Ms. Lee didn't push him. She sat quietly near the bed and read books aloud. Slowly, Milo came out and sat beside her. A month later, he began to sleep on her lap. Ms. Lee realized that it takes time to make a new friend, whether it is a person or a cat. Now Milo greets her at the door every day when she comes home from work.
23. What did Ms. Lee do when Milo was shy?
A. She pushed him out from under the bed.
B. She read quietly near him.
C. She sent him back.
D. She gave him a new bed.
=B 她靜靜坐在床邊大聲朗讀，沒有逼他。
24. What does the passage show?
A. Cats are not friendly.
B. Making friends takes time and patience.
C. Cats like to hide.
D. Reading helps cats sleep.
=B 文末說交朋友需要時間，無論是人或貓，也需要耐心。
## GROUP 25-27|對話|Choosing a Phone
Salesperson: Hello. May I help you?
Customer: I'm looking for a phone for my daughter. She's twelve.
Salesperson: What does she need it for?
Customer: Mainly to call me and to take pictures.
Salesperson: Then I recommend this one. It's $5,000 and has a very good camera.
Customer: Hmm, it's a little expensive. Do you have anything cheaper?
Salesperson: This model is $3,500. The camera is not as good, but it's easy to use.
Customer: I'll take that one. Does it come with a case?
Salesperson: Yes. It also has a one-year warranty.
25. Who is the phone for?
A. The customer.
B. The customer's husband.
C. The customer's daughter.
D. The salesperson.
=C 顧客要替十二歲的女兒買手機。
26. Why does the customer choose the $3,500 phone?
A. It has a better camera.
B. It is cheaper.
C. It is smaller.
D. It is on sale.
=B 顧客覺得 $5,000 的有點貴，改買較便宜的 $3,500 手機。
27. What does the $3,500 phone come with?
A. A free camera.
B. A free phone call.
C. A case and a one-year warranty.
D. A gift card.
=C 店員說附手機殼，還有一年保固。

Less than 1 hour: 10%

## GROUP 35-37|短文|The Library on Wheels
In a remote part of Colombia, there is a teacher named Luis who owns a very special library. It is a truck full of books. Every weekend, he drives it to small villages where children have never had enough books to read. At first, Luis used only a few books from his own bookshelf. Then people from other cities heard about him and began to send him more. Today, there are over 4,000 books on his truck. Luis says, "A book can take you anywhere. Even if you can't travel, you can see the whole world." The children wait for him every weekend with big smiles.
35. What does Luis do every weekend?
A. He teaches at a school.
B. He drives a truck of books to villages.
C. He writes children's books.
D. He sells books in the city.
=B 他每週末開著裝滿書的卡車到偏遠小村莊。
36. How did Luis get more books?
A. He bought them from a bookstore.
B. He borrowed them from a school.
C. People sent them to him.
D. He wrote them himself.
=C 其他城市的人聽說後開始寄書給他。
37. What does Luis mean by "A book can take you anywhere"?
A. Books are heavy to carry.
B. Reading lets you learn about the whole world.
C. Books can fly.
D. You can travel without books.
=B 他的意思是即使不能旅行，也能透過閱讀看見全世界。
