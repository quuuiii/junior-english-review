=SET 7|分冊複習・第3～4冊|過去簡單式與過去進行式、未來式、比較級與最高級、不定詞與動名詞、助動詞、現在完成式、被動語態、關係代名詞、間接問句、感官動詞、附加問句、連接詞
## SINGLE
1. We _____ in the middle of dinner when the lights went out.
A. were
B. are
C. have been
D. will be
=A when 子句用過去式 went out，當時的狀態用過去式 were（were in the middle of 表示正在……的過程中）。
2. Have you ever _____ a horse before?
A. ride
B. rode
C. ridden
D. riding
=C ever 搭配現在完成式，ride 的過去分詞是 ridden。
3. The movie was _____ than I thought. I almost fell asleep.
A. boring
B. more boring
C. most boring
D. the most boring
=B 後面有 than，用比較級 more boring。
4. My mother asked me _____ out the trash before dinner.
A. take
B. took
C. taking
D. to take
=D ask + 人 + to V。
5. This song is loved _____ many young people around the world.
A. by
B. for
C. with
D. in
=A 被動語態中動作的執行者用 by。
6. Judy has lived in Kaohsiung _____ she was born.
A. for
B. since
C. when
D. after
=B since 後面接過去的時間點或子句，與 has lived 搭配。
7. A: Did you go to the baseball game? B: No. I _____ to the game, but I was too busy.
A. would go
B. was going to go
C. went
D. have gone
=B was going to 表示過去原本打算做但沒做到的事。
8. Do you know when _____ for the trip?
A. will we leave
B. did we leave
C. we will leave
D. are we leaving
=C 間接問句用肯定句語序：疑問詞 + 主詞 + 動詞。
9. The man _____ lives next door is a famous painter.
A. which
B. who
C. whose
D. what
=B 先行詞是人 The man，作主詞用 who。
10. Mark was too tired _____ his homework last night.
A. finish
B. finishes
C. to finish
D. finishing
=C too ... to V 表示「太……而無法……」。
11. The cake looks _____, but I don't like sweet food.
A. deliciously
B. delicious
C. deliciousness
D. more deliciously
=B look 是連綴動詞，後面接形容詞 delicious。
12. You have to wear a helmet when you ride a motorcycle, _____ you?
A. don't
B. do
C. haven't
D. have
=A 前面是肯定句，附加問句用否定；have to 是一般動詞，用 don't you。
13. A: How long has your uncle _____ a teacher? B: For about twenty years.
A. be
B. been
C. being
D. was
=B has 後面接過去分詞 been。
14. When I was walking along the river, I _____ an old friend.
A. meet
B. meets
C. met
D. was meeting
=C 走路是持續中的動作，另一個短暫動作「遇見」用過去簡單式 met。
15. Jason is the _____ boy in my class. Nobody runs faster than him.
A. fast
B. faster
C. fastest
D. more fast
=C the + 最高級，沒有人比他快，所以是最快的。
16. The window is open. Who _____ it?
A. opens
B. opened
C. was opened
D. has been opened
=B 問「誰打開的」，主詞是 Who，用主動語態過去式 opened。
17. She sounds _____ on the phone. Maybe she has a cold.
A. tired
B. tiredly
C. tire
D. tiring
=A sound 是連綴動詞，後面接形容詞；tired 表示感到疲倦。
18. I'm sorry I'm late. I _____ the wrong bus.
A. take
B. am taking
C. took
D. will take
=C 說明遲到的原因是過去搭錯了車，用過去式 took。
19. A sign in front of a shop says: "Open from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m., seven days a week." What does it mean?
A. The shop is closed on Sundays.
B. The shop is open every day.
C. The shop opens at 9 p.m.
D. The shop is open for seven hours a day.
=B seven days a week 表示每天都營業。

## GROUP 25-27|對話|At the Train Station
Passenger: Excuse me. How can I get to the National Museum?
Officer: You can take the MRT Red Line and get off at Central Station. It's about twenty minutes.
Passenger: Is it far from the station to the museum?
Officer: No, it isn't. You can walk there in five minutes. Just go straight on Park Road and turn left at the bank.
Passenger: Thank you. By the way, how much is the ticket?
Officer: It's $30 for adults. But if you buy a day pass for $100, you can ride as many times as you want.
Passenger: I'm going to visit three places today. I think I'll buy the day pass.
Officer: Good idea!
25. How will the passenger go to the museum?
A. By taxi.
B. By bus.
C. By MRT.
D. On foot only.
=C 警員建議搭捷運紅線到中央車站。
26. Where should the passenger turn left?
A. At the museum.
B. At the bank.
C. At the station.
D. At the park.
=B 沿公園路直走，在銀行處左轉。
27. Why will the passenger buy the day pass?
A. It is cheaper than a ticket.
B. The officer told him to.
C. He will go to several places.
D. He doesn't have enough cash.
=C 他今天要去三個地方，所以買一日票。

## GROUP 35-37|短文|A Cup of Tea
There was once a young man named Leo. He was always in a hurry and never listened to others. One day he went to visit a wise old teacher. The teacher began to pour tea into Leo's cup. The cup became full, but the teacher kept pouring, and tea ran all over the table. "Stop! The cup is full!" Leo shouted. The teacher smiled and said, "Like this cup, your mind is full of your own ideas. If you want to learn something new, you must first make some room." Leo understood and bowed to the teacher.
35. What was Leo like at first?
A. He was kind and quiet.
B. He was always in a hurry.
C. He was a wise man.
D. He was a tea teacher.
=B 文中第二句說他總是匆匆忙忙，也不聽別人說話。
36. Why did the teacher keep pouring tea?
A. He wanted Leo to drink more tea.
B. He didn't see the cup was full.
C. He wanted to show Leo something.
D. He was angry at Leo.
=C 老師用倒茶的動作比喻，讓 Leo 明白道理。
37. What does the teacher want to tell Leo?
A. Tea is good for learning.
B. Leo should make room in his mind.
C. Leo should drink less tea.
D. The cup is too small.
=B 老師說想學新東西，必須先讓心中有空間。
## GROUP 38-43|填空|Learning Chinese
Jim is an American boy. He has been in Taiwan __38__ two years. At first, it was hard for him to learn Chinese because the characters __39__ very difficult. Many people helped him, and now he can talk with his classmates in Chinese. His teacher, __40__ is very kind, says he is making great progress. Last week, Jim was asked __41__ a speech in Chinese at school. He was so nervous __42__ he forgot some words. But everyone clapped for him. He is going to keep practicing so that he can speak Chinese __43__ than he does now.
38.
A. since
B. for
C. at
D. when
=B 後面接一段時間 two years，與 has been 搭配，用 for。
39.
A. look
B. looks
C. looking
D. looked
=D 「漢字看起來很難」，背景是過去，用過去式 looked 作連綴動詞。
40.
A. who
B. which
C. whose
D. whom
=A 先行詞是 His teacher，補充說明人並作主詞，用 who。
41.
A. give
B. to give
C. giving
D. gives
=B be asked to V，被要求做某事。
42.
A. that
B. because
C. if
D. but
=A so + 形容詞 + that 子句，表示「如此……以至於……」。
43.
A. well
B. good
C. better
D. best
=C 後面有 than，用比較級；well 的比較級是 better。
