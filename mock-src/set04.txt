=SET 4|分冊複習・第3冊|過去簡單式（一般動詞）、過去進行式、未來式（will／be going to）、比較級與最高級、不定詞、動名詞、助動詞（should／must／have to／may）、連接詞（when／because／if／before／after）、不規則動詞
## SINGLE
1. Last Sunday, my family _____ to the beach by car.
A. go
B. goes
C. went
D. going
=C Last Sunday 表示過去，go 的過去式是 went。
2. A: What were you doing at nine last night? B: I _____ a movie with my sister.
A. watch
B. watched
C. was watching
D. am watching
=C 過去某一時間點正在進行的動作，用過去進行式。
3. Look at those dark clouds. It _____ rain soon.
A. is going to
B. was going to
C. went to
D. goes to
=A 根據眼前的烏雲預測即將下雨，用 be going to，現在時間用 is。
4. Tom is _____ than his brother, but his brother runs faster.
A. tall
B. taller
C. tallest
D. the tallest
=B 兩人比較，用比較級 taller。
5. My grandmother wants _____ a trip to Japan next month.
A. take
B. takes
C. to take
D. taking
=C want 後面接不定詞 to + 原形動詞。
6. Mary enjoys _____ pictures of flowers in the park.
A. take
B. to take
C. took
D. taking
=D enjoy 後面接動名詞 V-ing。
7. You _____ eat too much candy. It's bad for your teeth.
A. shouldn't
B. should
C. may
D. can
=A 糖果對牙齒不好，所以「不應該」吃太多，用 shouldn't。
8. I was washing the dishes _____ the phone rang.
A. because
B. if
C. when
D. so
=C 洗碗的時候電話響了，用 when 連接兩個動作。
9. Jack didn't _____ his homework yesterday, so the teacher was angry.
A. do
B. did
C. does
D. doing
=A didn't 後面要接原形動詞 do。
10. Of the three boys, Peter is _____ student in our class.
A. good
B. better
C. the best
D. best
=C 三者以上的比較用 the + 最高級，good 的最高級是 the best。
11. We will have a picnic tomorrow if it _____ rain.
A. doesn't
B. won't
C. isn't
D. didn't
=A if 子句表示未來的條件時用現在式，所以用 doesn't rain。
12. A: Did you see Amy at the party? B: No, I _____.
A. don't
B. wasn't
C. didn't
D. haven't
=C 問句用 Did，簡答也用 didn't。
13. This movie is _____ interesting than that one. Let's watch it.
A. more
B. most
C. much
D. very
=A interesting 是多音節形容詞，比較級用 more interesting。
14. After he _____ breakfast, he went to school.
A. have
B. had
C. has
D. having
=B 過去的兩個連續動作，用過去式 had。
15. It is dangerous _____ in the river. You must not do that.
A. swim
B. swam
C. to swim
D. swims
=C It is + 形容詞 + to V，用不定詞 to swim。
16. A: Why was Ben late for school? B: _____ he missed the bus.
A. If
B. Because
C. But
D. Before
=B 回答原因，用 Because。
17. My sister bought a dress, but she _____ buy any shoes.
A. doesn't
B. isn't
C. wasn't
D. didn't
=D 前面 bought 是過去式，所以否定用 didn't。
18. Don't worry. I _____ help you with your English tomorrow.
A. will
B. did
C. was
D. am
=A tomorrow 表示未來，要用 will。
19. A sign in a park says: "Please do not feed the ducks." What does the sign mean?
A. You can give food to the ducks.
B. You must not give food to the ducks.
C. You have to feed the ducks.
D. You should take the ducks home.
=B do not feed 表示禁止餵鴨子。

## GROUP 25-27|對話|Planning a Trip
Lisa: Are you going to do anything this weekend, Ben?
Ben: Yes. I'm going to visit my grandparents in Tainan. How about you?
Lisa: I'm going to stay at home. My cat is sick, so I have to take her to the animal hospital.
Ben: I'm sorry to hear that. I hope she gets better soon.
Lisa: Thanks. Are you going to take the train to Tainan?
Ben: No. The train tickets are sold out. I'll take the bus. It takes about four hours.
Lisa: That's a long ride. You should bring some snacks and a book.
Ben: Good idea! I'll do that.
25. What is Ben going to do this weekend?
A. Take his cat to the hospital.
B. Visit his grandparents.
C. Stay at home.
D. Buy some train tickets.
=B Ben 說要去台南看祖父母。
26. Why will Ben take the bus?
A. The bus is faster.
B. The bus is cheaper.
C. He doesn't like the train.
D. The train tickets are sold out.
=D 火車票賣完了，所以搭巴士。
27. What does Lisa suggest Ben do?
A. Bring snacks and a book.
B. Go to Tainan by train.
C. Buy a gift for his grandparents.
D. Take the cat with him.
=A Lisa 說 You should bring some snacks and a book。

## GROUP 35-37|短文|The Best Pizza in Town
Mr. Rossi came to Taiwan from Italy ten years ago. At first, he worked in a hotel kitchen. But he missed the pizza from his hometown, so he decided to open his own pizza shop. It was not easy. He didn't have much money, and people in Taiwan didn't know much about real Italian pizza. Mr. Rossi worked fourteen hours a day, and he never gave up. Slowly, more and more people came to his shop. Today, his pizza is the most popular in the town. Mr. Rossi says, "If you work hard, your dream will come true."
35. Why did Mr. Rossi open a pizza shop?
A. He needed a job in a hotel.
B. He wanted to make money fast.
C. He missed the pizza in Italy.
D. He wanted to teach people Italian.
=C 他想念家鄉的披薩，所以決定開自己的披薩店。
36. Which of the following is true about Mr. Rossi?
A. He is from Taiwan.
B. He worked very hard.
C. He opened a shop in Italy.
D. He had a lot of money at first.
=B 他每天工作十四小時，從未放棄。
37. What does Mr. Rossi mean by "your dream will come true"?
A. You will be a rich person.
B. You will move to Italy.
C. You can get what you want by working hard.
D. You should open a pizza shop.
=C 他的意思是努力工作，夢想就能實現。
