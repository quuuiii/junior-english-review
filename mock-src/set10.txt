=SET 10|分冊複習・第5～6冊|分詞作形容詞、假設語氣、使役動詞、關係副詞（where／when）、複合關係代名詞 what、過去完成式、現在完成進行式、the more...the more...、名詞子句、分詞構句、感嘆句、so...that／such...that
## SINGLE
1. By the time we got to the station, the train _____.
A. left
B. has left
C. had left
D. leaves
=C 到達車站之前火車就已離開，先發生的動作用過去完成式 had left。
2. I have been _____ for you for two hours. Where were you?
A. wait
B. waited
C. waiting
D. waits
=C 現在完成進行式 have been + V-ing，強調動作從過去持續到現在。
3. This is the house _____ my grandfather was born.
A. which
B. who
C. where
D. what
=C 先行詞是地點 the house，關係副詞用 where。
4. _____ you need is a good rest. You have worked too hard.
A. That
B. What
C. Which
D. Who
=B What you need 是名詞子句作主詞，相當於 the thing that you need。
5. The more you practice, the _____ you will play.
A. good
B. well
C. better
D. best
=C the + 比較級 ..., the + 比較級 ...，well 的比較級是 better。
6. It was _____ a hot day that nobody wanted to go outside.
A. so
B. too
C. very
D. such
=D such + a + 形容詞 + 名詞 + that，表示「如此……以至於……」。
7. _____ the door, he found the room was empty.
A. Open
B. Opened
C. Opening
D. To open
=C 分詞構句，兩個動作由同一主詞完成，用現在分詞 Opening；即 When he opened the door。
8. I don't know _____ the meeting will start.
A. when
B. what
C. who
D. whose
=A 間接問句詢問時間，meeting will start 缺少時間，用 when。
9. If I had more time, I _____ travel around the world.
A. will
B. would
C. can
D. am going to
=B 與現在事實相反的假設，主要子句用 would + 原形動詞。
10. Peter was the _____ student in the class who got full marks.
A. one
B. only
C. alone
D. lonely
=B the only + 名詞，表示「唯一的」。
11. I remember the day _____ we first met.
A. what
B. where
C. when
D. which
=C 先行詞是時間 the day，關係副詞用 when。
12. Please wait here _____ I come back. I won't be long.
A. until
B. unless
C. during
D. since
=A until 表示「直到……為止」，請在這裡等到我回來。
13. How _____ the little girl is! Everyone loves her.
A. cute
B. a cute
C. cutely
D. cuteness
=A 感嘆句 How + 形容詞 + 主詞 + 動詞。
14. Because of the heavy rain, the baseball game was _____.
A. called off
B. called on
C. called up
D. called in
=A call off 表示取消。
15. Tina asked me _____ I had finished the report.
A. that
B. if
C. what
D. who
=B 間接問句表示「是否」，用 if。
16. I wish I _____ more money last year. I could have bought the bag.
A. have
B. had
C. had had
D. would have
=C 與過去事實相反的願望，wish 後面用過去完成式 had had。
17. _____ by the news, the girl started to cry.
A. Shocking
B. Shocked
C. Shock
D. To shock
=B 分詞構句表示被動，女孩是被消息震驚，用過去分詞 Shocked。
18. This book is worth _____. You will learn a lot from it.
A. read
B. to read
C. reading
D. be read
=C be worth + V-ing，值得做某事。
19. A sign in an elevator says: "In case of fire, use the stairs." What should you do when there is a fire?
A. Use the elevator.
B. Wait in the elevator.
C. Take the stairs.
D. Open the windows.
=C 發生火災時要走樓梯。

## GROUP 25-27|對話|Talking about a Trip
Ellie: How was your trip to Kyoto, Sam?
Sam: It was wonderful! I had always wanted to see the temples, and they were more beautiful than I had imagined.
Ellie: Did you try any local food?
Sam: Yes. We went to a small restaurant that was recommended by a friend. The tofu there was so delicious that I ate three bowls.
Ellie: Three bowls! What else did you do?
Sam: We rented kimonos and walked around the old streets. I took hundreds of photos.
Ellie: I'd love to see them. By the way, would you go again?
Sam: Definitely. If I had more vacation days, I would stay longer.
25. Why did Sam eat three bowls of tofu?
A. He was very hungry.
B. It was very cheap.
C. His friend ordered it.
D. It was so delicious.
=D 他說豆腐好吃到吃了三碗。
26. What did Sam do in the old streets?
A. He rented a car.
B. He wore kimonos.
C. He ate out every night.
D. He took the train.
=B 他們租了和服在老街散步。
27. What does Sam mean by "If I had more vacation days, I would stay longer"?
A. He has many vacation days.
B. He doesn't have enough vacation days.
C. He won't go back to Kyoto.
D. He stayed for too long.
=B 假設語氣表示與事實相反，事實是假期不夠多。
