=SET 14|會考模擬（四）|第1～6冊全範圍：詞彙、文法、對話、圖表與短文閱讀、綜合測驗
## SINGLE
1. The doctor told him to _____ smoking because it was bad for his lungs.
A. give up
B. take up
C. put up
D. look up
=A give up 表示放棄、戒除。
2. My little sister is too _____ to go to school alone. She is only five.
A. young
B. old
C. brave
D. tall
=A 她才五歲，年紀太小，不能自己上學。
3. It is _____ to keep a pet if you don't have enough time to take care of it.
A. polite
B. wise
C. unfair
D. useless
=C 沒有足夠時間照顧卻養寵物，對寵物不公平，所以選 unfair。
4. A: How was your trip to Japan? B: It was _____. I'd like to go there again.
A. terrible
B. awful
C. wonderful
D. boring
=C 回答想再去一次，表示旅行很棒。
5. The doctor said that smoking _____ cause serious diseases.
A. may
B. had to
C. were
D. used to
=A may 表示可能；其他選項在語意或文法上不通。
6. My father has _____ been to Europe. He wants to go this summer.
A. already
B. ever
C. never
D. just
=C 因為沒去過，所以想今年夏天去，用 never。
7. Sue is looking forward to _____ her cousin from Canada.
A. see
B. seeing
C. saw
D. be seen
=B look forward to 的 to 是介系詞，後面接動名詞。
8. The glass _____ when it fell off the table.
A. broke
B. was broken
C. has broken
D. breaks
=A 玻璃杯掉下桌子而碎了，是主動意義，用過去式 broke。
9. She is the kind of person _____ never gives up.
A. which
B. who
C. whose
D. what
=B 先行詞是 person，作主詞，用 who。
10. A: Are you free tomorrow? B: Yes, why do you ask? A: I wonder _____ you could help me with my homework.
A. that
B. if
C. what
D. who
=B wonder if 表示「想知道是否」。
11. The boy ran _____ the street without looking and almost got hit by a car.
A. across
B. along
C. among
D. between
=A run across the street 表示跑過街。
12. I'd rather _____ at home than go out in this weather.
A. stay
B. to stay
C. staying
D. stayed
=A would rather + 原形動詞。
13. Judy was _____ tired that she fell asleep during the movie.
A. such
B. so
C. too
D. very
=B so + 形容詞 + that 子句。
14. Do you know the girl _____ is talking to Mr. Brown?
A. whom
B. whose
C. which
D. who
=D 先行詞是人 the girl，作主詞，用 who。
15. Mom was cooking dinner _____ the phone rang.
A. while
B. when
C. after
D. since
=B 媽媽正在做飯，這時電話響了，用 when 連接長動作與短動作。
16. The mountain is _____ of snow in winter.
A. full
B. filled
C. fill
D. fulfilled
=A be full of 表示「充滿……」。
17. This road is closed _____ repairs. Please use the other one.
A. for
B. by
C. at
D. on
=A closed for repairs 表示因整修而封閉。
18. Neither Amy nor her sisters _____ at the party yesterday.
A. was
B. were
C. is
D. are
=B Neither...nor 的動詞與靠近的 her sisters（複數）一致，yesterday 是過去，用 were。
19. A poster at a school gate says: "Walk your bike inside the school." What should students do?
A. Ride their bikes fast.
B. Push their bikes.
C. Leave bikes at home.
D. Take bikes to class.
=B Walk your bike 表示牽著腳踏車走。

## GROUP 23-24|短文|Early Bird
Peter always went to school late. His teacher often scolded him, but he never changed. One day, he saw an old man who was sweeping the street early in the morning. The old man said, "I get up at four every day. When I work early, I have more time for myself later." This made Peter think. The next day, he got up half an hour earlier and found that he did not have to rush. He had time for breakfast and was never late again. He told his friends about it, and they started to get up earlier, too.
23. What did Peter learn from the old man?
A. Sweeping the street is fun.
B. Getting up early gives you more time.
C. Old people get up late.
D. Teachers like early birds.
=B 老人說早點工作，之後就有更多自己的時間。
24. What happened after Peter got up earlier?
A. He was late for school.
B. He lost his breakfast.
C. He had time for breakfast and was on time.
D. He quarreled with his friends.
=C 他有時間吃早餐，也不再遲到。
## GROUP 25-27|對話|Planning a Birthday Party
Nora: Dad's fiftieth birthday is next Sunday. Should we hold a party at home or at a restaurant?
Brian: A restaurant might be easier. We won't have to cook or clean.
Nora: But it'll cost more. And Dad prefers a quiet dinner at home.
Brian: You're right. How about ordering food from a restaurant and having it delivered?
Nora: Good idea! Then we can spend our time decorating the house.
Brian: I'll make a birthday cake. I've been practicing baking this month.
Nora: Really? That's great. Dad will be so happy.
Brian: Just don't tell him. I want it to be a surprise.
25. Why doesn't Nora want to hold the party at a restaurant?
A. It costs more and Dad likes quiet dinners at home.
B. Dad dislikes restaurant food.
C. The restaurant is too far.
D. The restaurant is closed on Sunday.
=A Nora 說餐廳花費較高，而且爸爸喜歡在家吃安靜的晚餐。
26. What will Brian do for the party?
A. Decorate the house.
B. Cook dinner.
C. Buy a gift.
D. Make a cake.
=D Brian 說他要做生日蛋糕。
27. What does Brian want?
A. To make the party a surprise.
B. To ask Dad to cook.
C. To invite many friends.
D. To change the date.
=A Brian 說不要告訴爸爸，要給他驚喜。
## GROUP 28-29|電子郵件|Library Card
To: Ms. Yang
From: Tony Huang
Subject: Lost library card
Dear Ms. Yang,
I lost my library card last Friday, and I cannot find it anywhere. I think I may have left it on the bus. I have a few books to return next week, and I also want to borrow some new ones. Could you please tell me how to get a new card? Do I need to pay for it? If so, how much is it? I'll come to the library this Saturday afternoon, if possible. Thank you for your help.
Tony Huang
28. What is Tony's problem?
A. He lost his library card.
B. He forgot to return books.
C. He broke a book.
D. He can't find the library.
=A 他弄丟了借書證。
29. What does Tony want to know?
A. How to return books.
B. When the library closes.
C. How to get a new card and how much it costs.
D. Which books are popular.
=C 他詢問如何補辦新卡以及是否要付費、費用多少。

## GROUP 35-37|短文|Words That Heal
A hospital in a small town decided to try something new. Next to every patient's bed, they put a small box. Visitors, nurses, and even strangers could write short messages on cards and put them in the box. The messages were simple: "Get well soon," "You are stronger than you think," and "We are thinking of you." The hospital found that patients who read these cards often felt less lonely and more hopeful. Some patients even said that the cards made them forget their pain for a while. Now, other hospitals are copying the idea. The director of the hospital says, "Medicine can heal the body, but kind words can heal the heart."
35. What did the hospital put next to every bed?
A. A television.
B. A small box for cards.
C. A telephone.
D. A flower.
=B 醫院在每位病人床邊放了一個放卡片的小箱子。
36. How did the cards help the patients?
A. They made patients feel less lonely.
B. They helped patients sleep.
C. They gave patients free medicine.
D. They made patients leave early.
=A 卡片讓病人比較不孤單，也更有希望。
37. What does the director mean?
A. Kind words are more useful than medicine.
B. Kind words can comfort people's hearts.
C. Patients should write more cards.
D. Doctors should talk less.
=B 院長的意思是醫藥治療身體，而善意的話語可以撫慰心靈。
## GROUP 38-43|填空|My Hometown
I come from a small town __38__ is known for its hot springs. Many tourists visit it every winter. When I was a child, I often went to the springs with my grandfather. He __39__ me many stories about the town. For example, the first hot spring was found by a farmer by accident. Today, the town has changed a lot. There are __40__ hotels and shops than before. Some people worry that too many tourists will hurt the environment. I think we should find a way to protect the town __41__ still welcoming visitors. If we work together, I believe we can solve this problem __42__. After all, a town is not only a place for tourists, but also a place __43__ people live.
38.
A. who
B. which
C. whose
D. what
=B 先行詞是物 a small town，作主詞，用 which。
39.
A. tells
B. told
C. telling
D. has told
=B 描述小時候的事，用過去式 told。
40.
A. many
B. more
C. most
D. much
=B 後面有 than，用比較級；hotels and shops 是複數，所以用 more。
41.
A. while
B. unless
C. because
D. so
=A while 在此表示「同時」，保護小鎮同時仍歡迎遊客。
42.
A. easy
B. easily
C. easier
D. ease
=B 修飾動詞 solve，用副詞 easily。
43.
A. which
B. where
C. what
D. who
=B 先行詞是地點 a place，關係副詞用 where。
