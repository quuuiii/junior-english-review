=SET 8|分冊複習・第1～4冊|第1～4冊綜合：各種時態（現在、過去、未來、進行、完成）、被動語態、關係代名詞、間接問句、比較級與最高級、不定詞與動名詞、助動詞、連接詞、附加問句
## SINGLE
1. Alice _____ in London for three years before she moved to Taipei.
A. lives
B. lived
C. has lived
D. is living
=B before she moved 表示過去的時間，主要子句也用過去式 lived。
2. The tickets for the concert _____ out in two hours yesterday.
A. sell
B. sold
C. were sold
D. was sold
=C 票是被賣掉的，主詞 tickets 是複數，過去被動用 were sold。
3. I'm not sure whether he _____ to the party tonight.
A. come
B. comes
C. coming
D. will come
=D tonight 表示未來，whether 子句用 will come。
4. Mom told us to stop _____ video games and go to bed.
A. play
B. played
C. playing
D. to play
=C stop + V-ing 表示停止正在做的事。
5. The girl _____ hair is very long is my classmate.
A. who
B. whose
C. which
D. that
=B 先行詞 The girl 與 hair 是所有關係，用 whose。
6. Neither Tom nor his parents _____ at home now.
A. is
B. was
C. are
D. be
=C Neither...nor 的動詞和靠近它的主詞一致，parents 是複數，現在式用 are。
7. A: Have you ever been to Tainan? B: Yes. I _____ there last summer.
A. go
B. went
C. have gone
D. have been
=B last summer 是明確的過去時間，不能與完成式連用，要用過去式 went。
8. Linda is _____ of all the girls in her class.
A. tall
B. taller
C. the tallest
D. more tall
=C 在全班女生中比較，用 the tallest。
9. If it _____ tomorrow, we will stay at home.
A. rains
B. rained
C. will rain
D. is raining
=A if 條件子句表示未來時，動詞用現在式 rains。
10. It was kind _____ you to help me move the boxes.
A. at
B. for
C. of
D. to
=C It is + 表示人品德的形容詞 + of + 人 + to V。
11. This isn't my pen. Mine _____ on the desk.
A. is
B. are
C. were
D. be
=A Mine 代表 my pen，是單數，現在式用 is。
12. The students were told _____ quiet during the test.
A. keep
B. keeps
C. to keep
D. keeping
=C be told to V，被告知要做某事。
13. This soup is _____ hot to drink. Please wait for a few minutes.
A. very
B. so
C. too
D. enough
=C too + 形容詞 + to V，表示「太……而不能……」。
14. How _____ has your sister been in Japan?
A. much
B. many
C. long
D. often
=C 問時間長度，用 How long。
15. You didn't bring your umbrella, _____?
A. did you
B. didn't you
C. do you
D. will you
=A 前面是否定句，附加問句用肯定形式；過去式用 did you。
16. The boy broke the vase _____ he was playing basketball in the house.
A. while
B. but
C. so
D. or
=A 打球的同時打破花瓶，表示同時發生的兩動作，用 while。
17. Be careful! The floor is wet. You may _____.
A. fall
B. falls
C. fell
D. falling
=A may 後面接原形動詞 fall。
18. The story _____ I read last night was about a brave dog.
A. who
B. what
C. which
D. whose
=C 先行詞是物 The story，作 read 的受詞，用 which。
19. A notice at a pool says: "Children under 120 cm must be with an adult." Tim is 110 cm tall. What must he do?
A. Swim alone.
B. Go with an adult.
C. Wait until he is older.
D. Buy a ticket.
=B 低於 120 公分的孩子必須有大人陪同，Tim 才 110 公分。

## GROUP 35-37|短文|The Honest Boy
Ken found a wallet on the street on his way home. There was a lot of money in it, and he was tempted to keep it. He had wanted to buy a new pair of shoes for a long time, but his family didn't have much money. He thought for a while and then took the wallet to the police station. The next day, the owner, an old man, came to Ken's house to thank him. He wanted to give Ken some money, but Ken said no. "I only did what I should do," he said. The old man was moved and told Ken's story to the newspaper. Soon, many people knew about Ken's kindness.
35. Why was Ken tempted to keep the wallet?
A. He didn't have a wallet.
B. His family didn't have much money.
C. He wanted to buy a gift for the old man.
D. The wallet was very nice.
=B 他很想買新鞋，但家裡沒什麼錢。
36. What did Ken do with the wallet?
A. He kept it.
B. He gave it to a friend.
C. He took it to the police station.
D. He took it to the newspaper.
=C 他把皮夾送到警察局。
37. What did the old man do after he met Ken?
A. He gave Ken some shoes.
B. He told Ken's story to the newspaper.
C. He took Ken to dinner.
D. He sent Ken a letter.
=B 老人很感動，把肯的故事告訴了報社。
